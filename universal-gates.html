Universal Gate Circuits

It turns out that the NAND and NOR gates are functionally complete, in that any logical function can be implemented by combining only, one or more NAND gates, or one or more NOR gates. This theorem is useful because standardizing on just one type of logic gate can leverage economies of scale by simplifying inventory.
