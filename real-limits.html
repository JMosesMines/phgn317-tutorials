Real-world limits

Fan-out

How many gates can one gate drive? This is the question of fan-out. Ideally the limit would be infinite, but in practice it is not. The maximum fan-out depends on the current driving capacity on the output side of a logic gate, and the current consumption requirements of the inputs.

Propagation delay

Propagation delay is the time required for a signal change at a gate's input to result in a corresponding change at its output
