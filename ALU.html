Arithmetic Logic Unit

An arithmetic logic unit (ALU) uses a combination of logic gates to perform any of several available digital operations. The inputs are the operands, and an opcode. The opcode selects what operation should be performed on the operands.

The 74181 is a relatively simple classic example of a standalone ALU IC. Look over its data sheet, here.
